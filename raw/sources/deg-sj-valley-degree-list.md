## Key facts (paraphrased)

- **Lodge of Perfection, 4°–14°** ("ineffable" degrees): loyalty, devotion to truth and the search for what was lost, building on the Blue Lodge. 4 Secret Master · 5 Perfect Master · 6 Intimate Secretary · 7 Provost and Judge · 8 Intendant of the Building · 9 Elu of the Nine · 10 Elu of the Fifteen · 11 Elu of the Twelve · 12 Master Architect · 13 Royal Arch of Solomon · 14 Perfect Elu.
- **Chapter of Rose Croix, 15°–18°:** faith, hope and charity; universal religious philosophy. 15 Knight of the East · 16 Prince of Jerusalem · 17 Knight of the East and West · 18 Knight Rose Croix.
- **Council of Kadosh, 19°–30°:** chivalric and higher degrees on justice, liberty and truth. 19 Grand Pontiff · 20 Master of the Symbolic Lodge · 21 Noachite, or Prussian Knight · 22 Knight of the Royal Axe · 23 Chief of the Tabernacle · 24 Prince of the Tabernacle · 25 Knight of the Brazen Serpent · 26 Prince of Mercy · **27 Knight Commander of the Temple · 28 Knight of the Sun** · 29 Knight of St. Andrew · 30 Knight Kadosh.
- **Consistory, 31°–32°:** 31 Inspector Inquisitor · 32 Master of the Royal Secret (highest degree regularly conferred).
- **Honors, not applied for:** KCCH (Knight Commander of the Court of Honor) for service; **33° Inspector General Honorary**, by invitation of the Supreme Council only.
- The page stresses that Scottish Rite degrees *expand on* the Master Mason degree; they are not "higher" than it.
