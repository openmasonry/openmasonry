# Clip: Feichtinger, Fritsch, Vonlanthen, Wattenhofer (2023), "The Hidden Shortcomings of (D)AOs: An Empirical Study of On-Chain Governance"

- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.12125 (PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2302.12125)
- **Authors:** Rainer Feichtinger, Robin Fritsch, Yann Vonlanthen, Roger Wattenhofer (ETH Zürich)
- **Date:** February 2023; published in Financial Cryptography 2023 Workshops (CoDecFin), Springer LNCS 13953, pp. 165–185.
- **Accessed:** November 2026 (via abstract and index pages)

## Key findings (paraphrased)

- Empirical study of on-chain governance in **21 DAOs**; dataset released openly.
- **Concentration:** in **17 of 21** DAOs, fewer than **10 participants** control a majority of voting power, enough to decide any vote.
- **Delegation** drives concentration: most voting power sits with delegates who mostly represent a single token holder.
- **Participation is thin:** little evidence of substantial community participation in decisions.
- **Costs:** on-chain costs of delegating, voting and transferring tokens add up to millions of dollars in some DAOs.
- A slight trend toward decentralization over time is noted, but it does not change the overall picture.
