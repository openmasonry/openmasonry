## Key facts (paraphrased)

- The Ancient and Accepted Scottish Rite (AASR) confers degrees 4–32 after the three Craft degrees; the 33rd is conferred by a Supreme Council.
- **Southern Jurisdiction (SJ):** founded in Charleston, South Carolina, on May 31, 1801 (John Mitchell and Frederick Dalcho among the founders; first "Supreme Council of the Thirty-third Degree"). Calls itself the Mother Supreme Council of the World.
- **Northern Masonic Jurisdiction (NMJ):** chartered August 5, 1813 by Emanuel De La Motta; first Sovereign Grand Commander Daniel D. Tompkins; inactive 1832–1845.
- **Albert Pike** (b. Boston, Dec 29, 1809): received degrees 4–32 from Albert Mackey in March 1853; revised the SJ rituals (first revision 1857, completed 1884); Sovereign Grand Commander from January 1859; published *Morals and Dogma* in 1871.
- The 1762 Constitutions and Regulations (Rite of Perfection, 25 degrees) are cited as a precursor; the "Grand Constitutions of 1786" attributed to Frederick the Great are treated as of doubtful authenticity.
