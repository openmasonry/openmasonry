# Clip: Vitalik Buterin, "Moving beyond coin voting governance"

- **URL:** https://vitalik.eth.limo/general/2021/08/16/voting3.html
- **Author:** Vitalik Buterin (personal blog)
- **Date:** 2021-08-16
- **Accessed:** November 2026
- **Copyright:** summarized in my own words; one short quote.

## Key arguments (paraphrased)

- Decentralized governance is needed (funding public goods, coordinating upgrades) but today's dominant form, **coin voting**, is dangerous.
- **Plutocracy / collusion of whales:** large holders coordinate easily; small holders have negligible influence.
- **Low participation (rational apathy):** a small holder's vote almost never matters, so they neither vote nor research proposals.
- **Misaligned incentives:** only coin holders vote, favoring short-term price over the wider community; rent extraction becomes possible.
- **Vote buying:** voting rights can be separated from economic exposure (lending markets, wrapper tokens, exchange custody), so an attacker can rent votes cheaply; time locks help only weakly.
- **Alternatives:** limit what token votes can decide; **proof of personhood** (one human, one vote); **proof of participation** (weight by contribution); **skin in the game** (futarchy, forks that penalize coins that voted for harmful decisions, reputation); hybrids such as advisory coin votes with time delays.
- Short quote: "Coin voting is attractive because it feels credibly neutral" (Buterin, 2021).
