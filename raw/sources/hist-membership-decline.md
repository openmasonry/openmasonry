# Clip: Membership decline in the UGLE (England & Wales) and the United States (MSANA)

## Sources clipped
1. Masonic Service Association of North America (MSANA), "Jurisdictional Totals": https://msana.com/services/jurisdictional-totals/ (accessed November 2026). MSANA has compiled US Grand Lodge membership since 1924/25; each Grand Lodge counts members differently.
2. 1959 peak figure as reported from MSANA data by secondary sources (e.g. "Building Hiram" blog, January 2026; phoenixmasonry.org membership statistics page; certificate expired at capture).
3. UGLE, *Annual Report 2023/2024* (via https://www.ugle.org.uk/about-us/annual-reports and press coverage); *UGLE Annual Report 2021* (issuu.com/freemasonrytoday/docs/ugle_annual_report_2021). Accessed November 2026.
4. *Freemasonry Today*, Spring 2023 (issuu): statement on long-term decline.
5. UGLE website navigation: "around 170,000 members throughout England and Wales, as well as Districts overseas" (accessed November 2026).

## United States (members of mainstream Grand Lodges, MSANA)
| Year | Members |
|---|---|
| 1959 (peak) | 4,103,161 (secondary sources citing MSANA) |
| 2018 | 1,046,846 |
| 2019 | 1,010,150 |
| 2020 | 942,195 |
| 2021 | 919,129 |
| 2022 | 881,219 |
| 2023 | 869,429 |

- Fall of ~79% from 1959 to 2023; ~17% from 2018 to 2023 (~3.6%/yr). A blog projects ~624,000 by 2036 at ~3%/yr (projection, not data).
- Prince Hall Grand Lodges are counted separately and not included in these totals.
- Conflicting estimate: one popular site claims "just under 2 million" US Freemasons; not MSANA-based, so disregarded.

## United Grand Lodge of England
| Period | Total | Home (England, Wales, Channel Is., Isle of Man) | Overseas districts |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2021 (calendar) | 175,657 | 156,876 | 18,781 |
| 2023/24 (Apr–Mar) | 168,961 | 150,124 | 18,837 |

- *Freemasonry Today* (2023): about **2.5% annual decline since 2008** in the home areas, from around 200,000 to a little more than 150,000.
- Note: the reporting periods differ (calendar year vs April–March).
- Historical: about 647 lodges in 1814 and 2,800 in 1900 (UGLE history page).
- Responses: UGLE halved membership fees for under-25s (2024) and reported rising membership enquiries after its openness campaigns.

## Other figures gathered (for context)
- Grand Orient de France: ~52,000–55,000 members (Wikipedia, 2025).
