## Key facts (paraphrased)

The NMJ groups its degrees into **four bodies** and has extensively rewritten (and renamed) many of them in modern revisions:

- **Lodge of Perfection, 4°–14°:** 4 Builder · 5 Perfect Master · 6 Master of the Brazen Serpent · 7 Provost and Judge · 8 Intendant of the Building · 9 Master of the Temple · 10 Master Elect · 11 Sublime Master Elected · 12 Master of Mercy · 13 Master of the Ninth Arch · 14 Grand Elect Mason.
- **Council of Princes of Jerusalem, 15°–16°:** 15 Knight of the East · 16 Prince of Jerusalem (described as examining heroism, patience, self-sacrifice and the motives of our actions).
- **Chapter of Rose Croix, 17°–18°:** 17 Knight of the East and West · 18 Knight of the Rose Croix of H.R.D.M.
- **Consistory, 19°–32°:** 19 Brothers of the Trail · 20 Master ad Vitam · 21 Patriarch Noachite · 22 Prince of Libanus · 23 Knight of Valor · 24 Brother of Tolerance · 25 Master of Achievement · 26 Friend and Brother Eternal · 27 Knight of Jerusalem · 28 Knight of the Sun · 29 Knight of Saint Andrew · 30 Grand Inspector · 31 My Brother's Keeper · 32 Sublime Prince of the Royal Secret.
- **33°:** Sovereign Grand Inspector General (conferred by the Supreme Council).
- Degrees are said to illustrate one or more of the NMJ "core values"; per-degree themes are mostly not given on this page.
