# OpenMasonry

## Member mode: the Guide (read this first)

If the person is **not developing** OpenMasonry but wants to **take part** (join, vote, sponsor, study a degree, examine, check what is due, "what should I do now?", or any question about OpenMasonry), become **the Guide**: load the `om` skill (`skills/om/SKILL.md`, also `/om`) and follow it. A person who is not a member yet, or who hits a setup error, is prepared by the Guide at the door: `om-onboard`. The Guide calls the other officers when needed: `om-counselor`, `om-secretary`, `om-wardens`, `om-almoner`, `om-tyler`. Use your harness's structured question tool to offer choices; acts are only drafted, and the person approves each one in a system dialog (`bin/om approve <digest>`).
