# Maria Deraismes (1828–1894)

French writer, public speaker and campaigner for women's rights and secularism. She was the first woman initiated into a male Masonic lodge with the same ritual as men, and co-founder of [[le-droit-humain]].

## Facts
- **Jan 14, 1882:** initiated by the lodge *Les Libres Penseurs* of Le Pecq, which declared itself autonomous from its grand lodge in order to do so. The grand lodge did not recognize the act. Most sources name the Symbolic Scottish Grand Lodge; one mentions the Grand Orient, and the point **remains unresolved**.
- **Apr 4, 1893** (some sources give Mar 14): with the physician and senator Georges Martin, she initiates a group of women and founds the *Grande Loge Symbolique Écossaise Mixte de France "Le Droit Humain"* (Mixed Symbolic Scottish Grand Lodge of France), the world's first **mixed** Masonic order. She becomes its first Grand Master.
- She dies in February 1894, a few months after the founding.

## Why it matters
- She showed that the exclusion of women, written into Charge III of 1723 ([[james-anderson]]), was **convention**, not essence. All it took was one lodge willing to break away.
- The price was the loss of recognition. Mixed Freemasonry remains outside Anglo-American "regularity". See [[regularity-and-recognition]].

## For OpenMasonry
She is the direct forerunner of Landmark I ("The Open Door"). OpenMasonry goes further by making non-discrimination an **unamendable** landmark, protected by the verifier rather than by the goodwill of a single lodge.
