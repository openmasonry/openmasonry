# Propaganda Due (P2) Lodge

An Italian lodge, originally under the Grand Orient of Italy (GOI), which under Licio Gelli became a **secret network of parallel power**. Its discovery in 1981 set off one of the biggest political scandals of republican Italy.

## Facts
- **1877:** founded in Turin as *Propaganda Massonica*, a lodge for prominent members with confidential membership. Closed by Fascism in 1925 and renumbered "Propaganda Due" after the war.
- **1970s:** Licio Gelli (1919–2015) takes control and turns it into a personal instrument. The GOI **withdraws its charter in 1976**, although sources vary between 1974 and 1981 for the suspension and expulsion. The lodge carries on clandestinely and **irregularly**.
- **March 1981:** the list of **962 names** seized from Gelli includes ministers, members of parliament, the heads of all the secret services, generals, judges, bankers and journalists. The government falls.
- **Anselmi Commission (1981–1984):** concludes that P2 infiltrated state institutions in order to condition political life.
- **Law 17/1982:** dissolves P2 and bans secret associations in Italy.
- It was linked to Banco Ambrosiano, to the death of Roberto Calvi (1982) and, according to recent court rulings, to the financing of the Bologna massacre (1980).
- **Disputed points:** whether everyone on the list was actually a member, and how far the GOI leadership tolerated the lodge before breaking with it.

## The correct reading
P2 **does not prove** that "Freemasonry" is conspiratorial. Its own Grand Orient had repudiated it. What it proves is that **secrecy + patronage + permanence**, with no external audit, create a network that can be captured by anyone who profits from it. This is the whitepaper's central thesis (§1.3).
