# Lodge

The basic unit of Freemasonry. The word has two senses: the organized group of members and the room where they meet. In operative masonry the *lodge* was the building, usually temporary and often little more than a shed, beside the works, where the masons kept their tools, ate and organized themselves. The name passed from the shed to the men.

**Traditional readings**
- **Image of the world.** For Mackey the lodge has the form of an "oblong square", the known world of the ancients. Its ceiling is the sky, and its officers allude to the course of the sun. Haywood calls it a "mystical representation of the world".
- **Universal brotherhood.** Members of every condition, gathered in harmony, prefigure a universal brotherhood.
- **School of government.** For historians such as Margaret Jacob, the 18th-century lodge was a laboratory of self-government, with a constitution, elections, minutes and representatives. See [[enlightenment-sociability]].
- **Internal government.** Since Mackey's landmarks, the lodge is presided over by a Master (the Worshipful Master) and two Wardens and is "tyled", that is, guarded at the door (see [[tyler]]).

## In OpenMasonry
- A **Lodge** is a group of at least 7 members who share a rhythm of study, online or in person (whitepaper §10.1). In v0.1 everyone belongs to **Open Lodge No. 1**. From v0.5 each Lodge gets its own namespace and repository, federated by a 19th-degree Pontifex (charge `bridge`).
- **Offices:** Worshipful Master (3rd+), two Wardens (3rd+), Secretary, Treasurer (relief fund only, Landmark III) and Steward of Light (mentoring). Terms are 1 year, renewable once, and elections follow the amendment rule (see [[decision-function]]).
- The corresponding symbolic house is "The Craft Lodge" (degrees 1–3: self-knowledge and the tools of the work).
- The lodge as a world is now literal and open: GitHub Discussions are "the Lodge" (the social layer, §4.1).
- A Lodge is not a **room**. Each degree also has a study room, closed but never secret: a place of conversation that decides nothing (Constitution Art. XV, whitepaper §10.6). The rooms open at the public launch.
- The nesting Lodge → House → Supreme Council fulfills Ostrom's 8th principle (see [[ostrom-principles]]).
