# Regularity and Recognition

Two distinct but linked concepts.
- **Regularity** is a grand lodge's conformity to criteria held to be essential.
- **Recognition** is the sovereign, bilateral act by which one Grand Lodge accepts another as regular and allows members to visit each other's lodges.

The reference standard is the *Basic Principles* published by the [[united-grand-lodge-of-england]] in 1929: legitimate origin, belief in the Supreme Being, the Volume of the Sacred Law, men only, sovereignty, the Three Great Lights, no religion or politics in lodge, and observance of the [[landmarks]].

**Historical consequences**
- In 1877 the Grand Orient de France dropped the requirement of belief in the [[great-architect-of-the-universe]] and has since been treated as irregular by the UGLE. Since then Freemasonry has been split into a "regular" current and a "liberal" or "adogmatic" one.
- Mixed and women's grand lodges (Le Droit Humain, 1893) exist, but are not recognized by the regular current.
- Prince Hall Freemasonry, founded by Black Americans refused by colonial lodges, waited until the late 20th century for broad recognition.

**Readings**
- **Defensive:** regularity protects identity and stops any group from calling itself "Freemasonry".
- **Critical:** it works as a legitimacy cartel, in which insiders decide who may enter and exclusions become the norm.

## In OpenMasonry
- OpenMasonry declares its **independence**: it is not affiliated with, recognized by or a spokesperson for any grand lodge (whitepaper §12.1). Members of traditional grand lodges are welcome and remain bound by their own obligations.
- Legitimacy is not granted by another institution. It is **reproducible**: anyone can replay the ledger from genesis and obtain the same state (Landmark VI; see [[chained-ledger]]). Landmark VII: "Legitimacy is not owned. It is earned in public."
- The criteria that exclude in the tradition (sex, belief) are exactly the ones Landmark I forbids. The 29th degree, Knight of Saint Andrew, has the duty to raise an equality objection: to "refer to the Court of Review any rule that refuses or ranks anyone for sex, gender, origin, color, belief or unbelief, class or disability" (`canon/degrees.toml`, 29°). See [[eternal-landmarks-of-openmasonry]] and [[openmasonry-vs-traditional-freemasonry]].
- "Recognition" between OpenMasonry's own Lodges will be verifiable **federation** (v0.5, the 19th-degree Pontifex), not a discretionary act.
