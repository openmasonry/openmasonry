# AASR: Southern Jurisdiction vs. Northern Jurisdiction

In the USA, the Scottish Rite has **two Supreme Councils**:

- **Southern Jurisdiction (SJ):** headquartered in Washington, D.C. Founded in Charleston in 1801, it is the "Mother Council". It covers 35 states and territories. See [[supreme-council-aasr-southern-jurisdiction]].
- **Northern Masonic Jurisdiction (NMJ):** chartered in 1813. It covers 15 states in the Northeast and Midwest.

Both share the 33-degree numbering but differ in their **bodies** and in many **names**. The NMJ rewrote much of its degree work in modern revisions. The SJ essentially keeps the structure revised by [[albert-pike]]. OpenMasonry takes the **SJ** as its historical reference.

## Reading

- **SJ:** conservative in its text and close to Pike (*Morals and Dogma*, 1871). It keeps the Temple legend and the "Elu" series.
- **NMJ:** modernized several degrees with new morality plays, some set in American history (for example, the 19° *Brothers of the Trail*), and tied them to "core values". The names change, but the numbering stays.
- **Order of the 27°/28°:** the Wikipedia table puts *Knight of the Sun* at 27°, but Pike (1884) and current SJ valleys put *Knight Commander of the Temple* at 27°. `canon/degrees.toml` follows the current SJ, and that order is correct.
