# Source: "The Hidden Shortcomings of (D)AOs" (ETH Zürich, 2023)

An empirical study of the on-chain governance of **21 DAOs**, published in the Financial Cryptography 2023 workshops.

## Findings

- **Concentration:** in 17 of the 21 DAOs, **fewer than 10 participants** hold the majority of voting power, enough to decide any vote.
- **Delegation concentrates power:** most power sits with delegates who generally represent a single large holder.
- **Little effective participation** by the community.
- **Cost:** delegating, voting and transferring tokens on-chain cost millions of dollars in some DAOs.

## Relation to OpenMasonry

The study gives empirical grounding to the argument in [[openmasonry-vs-token-daos]]. In OpenMasonry voting is free (one Git commit), every vote weighs the same, and [[dormancy]] removes inactive members from the quorum denominator.
