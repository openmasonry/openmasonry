# Ostrom, *Governing the Commons* (1990)

A book by [[elinor-ostrom]] (Nobel Prize in Economics, 2009). From field studies of long-lived commons, Ostrom derives **eight design principles**:
1. clear boundaries;
2. rules fitted to local conditions;
3. collective choice;
4. accountable monitoring;
5. graduated sanctions;
6. cheap conflict resolution;
7. the right to organize;
8. nested levels.

**Why it matters.** The whitepaper (§3.2) explicitly maps each principle to a protocol mechanism. See [[ostrom-principles]].
