# Source: Valley of Wichita (AASR, Southern Jurisdiction), "The Degrees"

The official page of a **valley** (local body) under the SJ Supreme Council. The central site, scottishrite.org, refused automated reading (HTTP 403), so this is the closest official list. It was cross-checked against the Valley of Omaha.

## Key points

- **Four bodies:** Lodge of Perfection (4–14), Chapter of Rose Croix (15–18), Council of Kadosh (19–30), Consistory (31–32).
- Confirms **27° Knight Commander of the Temple** and **28° Knight of the Sun**, the same order as `canon/degrees.toml`.
- The **33° is honorary** and comes only by invitation: "Inspector General Honorary", conferred by the Supreme Council for outstanding service. It is never requested. There is also the KCCH distinction (Knight Commander of the Court of Honor).
- Insists that the Scottish Rite degrees **expand** the Master Mason degree and do not sit "above" it. This is the same thesis as OpenMasonry's: the 3° is already a full member ([[catalog-of-the-33-degrees]]).
