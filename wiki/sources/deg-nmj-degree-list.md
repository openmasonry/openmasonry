# Source: NMJ Supreme Council, "Degrees"

The official list of degrees of the **Northern Masonic Jurisdiction** of the USA.

## Key points

- **Four bodies, with ranges different from the SJ's:** Lodge of Perfection (4–14), **Council of Princes of Jerusalem (15–16)**, Chapter of Rose Croix (17–18) and **Consistory (19–32)**. The NMJ has no separate Council of Kadosh.
- Many degrees were **renamed and rewritten** in modern revisions. Examples: 4° *Builder*, 6° *Master of the Brazen Serpent*, 9° *Master of the Temple*, 19° *Brothers of the Trail*, 23° *Knight of Valor*, 24° *Brother of Tolerance*, 25° *Master of Achievement*, 26° *Friend and Brother Eternal*, 27° *Knight of Jerusalem*, 30° *Grand Inspector*, 31° *My Brother's Keeper*, 32° *Sublime Prince of the Royal Secret*.
- Only the 16° has its theme described on the page: heroism, patience and self-denial.
