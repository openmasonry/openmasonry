# Source: Wikipedia on Masonic rites

Several Wikipedia articles, cross-checked against each other. Only facts consistent across the pages were kept.

## Key points

- **York Rite (American):** not a single body but three autonomous bodies. The **Royal Arch Chapter** confers Mark Master, Past Master, Most Excellent Master and Royal Arch Mason. The **Cryptic Council** confers Royal, Select and Super Excellent Master. The **Commandery of Knights Templar** confers Red Cross, Malta and Temple. The Templars require Christian faith.
- **French Rite:** grew out of the ritual of the London "Moderns" and was codified by the Grand Orient de France between 1781 and 1786. It has 3 Craft (Blue Lodge) degrees and 4 Orders of Wisdom, i.e. 7 degrees. Some Masonic bodies add a 5th Order. In 1877 the Grand Orient de France stopped requiring belief in God.
- **Swedish Rite:** Scandinavian and Christian, with 10 degrees (an 11th, honorary, in some jurisdictions).
- **Rectified Scottish Rite:** shaped by Jean-Baptiste Willermoz at Lyon (1778) and Wilhelmsbad (1782); Christian and chivalric.
