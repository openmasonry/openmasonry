# Jacob (1991)

A summary of a work of historiography.
- **Margaret C. Jacob**, *Living the Enlightenment* (1991): 18th-century lodges were laboratories of modern civil society, with constitutions, elections and representatives in miniature.

**Why it matters.** It is the historical thesis the whitepaper (§1.1) uses to say what Freemasonry got right. See [[enlightenment-sociability]] and [[freemasonry-timeline]].

**Limits.** It is a copyrighted work, so it appears here only in summary. Some critics liken Jacob to Habermas and point to the book's political emphasis.
