# Source: the decline in numbers

Membership figures for Freemasonry in the United States, in decline since the peak of 1959, and in England.

## United States (MSANA, regular Grand Lodges)
| Year | Members |
|---|---|
| 1959 (peak) | 4,103,161 (secondary sources citing the MSANA) |
| 2018 | 1,046,846 |
| 2020 | 942,195 |
| 2023 | 869,429 |

The drop is about **79%** since 1959 and about 3.6% a year between 2018 and 2023. Prince Hall Grand Lodges are **not included** in these totals, and each Grand Lodge counts members in its own way.

## England and Wales (UGLE)
| Period | Total | Home base |
|---|---|---|
| 2021 | 175,657 | 156,876 |
| 2023/24 | 168,961 | 150,124 |

*Freemasonry Today* magazine (2023) speaks of a decline of about **2.5% a year since 2008**, from about 200,000 to just over 150,000 in the home base.

## Contradiction with the whitepaper (resolved)
Caution: a figure sometimes quoted is "about 200,000 **a decade earlier**". The source says "since **2008**", i.e. about 15 years. Moreover, the 200,000 figure refers to the home base, and the 169,000 figure to the total including overseas districts. The whitepaper gives the home-base figures with their dates.
