# Entry "Great Architect of the Universe"

An encyclopedia entry tracing the image of God as architect, from Thomas Aquinas to Calvin (*Institutes*, 1536). According to the entry, [[james-anderson]] brought the expression into the *Book of Constitutions* (1723).

**Key points.** The term works as a **common denominator**: each member fills it with his or her own faith. The entry lists Christian, Gnostic, Hermetic and Rosicrucian readings, and mentions the "triune" variant of the Swedish Rite. See [[great-architect-of-the-universe]].

**Limits.** It is a collaborative entry. The Calvin → Anderson link is plausible but not directly documented.
