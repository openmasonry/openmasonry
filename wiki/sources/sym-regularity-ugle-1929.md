# UGLE Basic Principles (1929)

A summary of the criteria the [[united-grand-lodge-of-england]] published in 1929 for recognizing other Grand Lodges as "regular". Eight criteria are usually counted:
- legitimate origin;
- belief in the Supreme Being;
- the Volume of the Sacred Law;
- men only;
- sovereignty;
- the Three Great Lights;
- no religion or politics in lodge;
- observance of the landmarks.

**Why it matters.** Regularity is the mechanism that draws the line between "inside" and "outside" traditional Freemasonry, and recognition is a sovereign, bilateral act. The case of the Grand Orient of France (1877) shows how a doctrinal divergence turns into mutual exclusion. See [[regularity-and-recognition]].

**Limits.** The primary text was not recovered in full. Wording and count vary among summaries.
