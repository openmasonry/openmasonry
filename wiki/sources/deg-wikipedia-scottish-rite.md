# Source: Wikipedia, "Scottish Rite"

An encyclopedic overview of the **Ancient and Accepted Scottish Rite (AASR)** in the USA. Used as a map, cross-checked against the jurisdictions' own pages ([[deg-sj-valley-degree-list]], [[deg-nmj-degree-list]]).

## What it covers

- **Southern Jurisdiction (SJ):** founded in Charleston on 1801-05-31, the first Supreme Council of the 33rd degree, called the "Mother Council of the World" (see [[supreme-council-aasr-southern-jurisdiction]]).
- **Northern Jurisdiction (NMJ):** chartered on 1813-08-05; inactive between 1832 and 1845.
- **[[albert-pike]]:** received degrees 4–32 from Albert Mackey in 1853, revised the SJ rituals between 1857 and 1884, was Sovereign Grand Commander from 1859 and published *Morals and Dogma* (1871).
- Precursor: the 25-degree Rite of Perfection (Regulations of 1762). The "Grand Constitutions of 1786", attributed to Frederick the Great, are considered of doubtful authenticity.
- Lists the 33 SJ degree names, which match `canon/degrees.toml` **except for the order of 27 and 28**: the Wikipedia table puts *Knight of the Sun* at 27 and *Knight Commander of the Temple* at 28. Pike (1884) and current SJ valley pages give the reverse order, which is the one the canon uses.
