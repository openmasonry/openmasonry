# Symbols and Virtues of the 33 Degrees

A table that sets out, for each of the 33 OpenMasonry degrees, the **virtue** the degree asks for, the **art** it studies, the **heraldic charge** of its coat of arms (the `charge` field in `canon/degrees.toml`, with tincture and ordinary) and the **traditional symbol** it echoes. Titles, virtues and arts follow the English canon. The normative source remains `canon/degrees.toml`, and the full catalog is at [[catalog-of-the-33-degrees]].

**How to read the last column.** "Echo" is an interpretive reading: it names the image from Masonic, biblical or heraldic tradition that the charge evokes, with links to the concept pages. It does not claim that the corresponding Scottish Rite degree uses that emblem in the same way, and it reproduces no reserved ritual content.

## Patterns the table reveals
- **The mason's tools open and close the Craft.** Rough ashlar (1°), level (2°) and compasses (3°) follow the traditional progression of rough-dressing, squaring and setting the stone. The plumb and the square return in the 8° and 12°, tied to maintenance and design. See [[twenty-four-inch-gauge-and-gavel]].
- **Tinctures follow the Houses, without a rigid rule.** The Craft begins in argent (silver, the raw stone) and the 3° moves to azure. Sable (black) marks discretion and memory (4°, 5°, 21°, 30°). Or (gold) marks the apex (33°) and degrees of search and truth (13°, 14°, 16°, 23°, 28°).
- **Symbols of power become symbols of limit.** The double-headed eagle (32°) teaches that "the royal secret is that no one should hold it alone." The delta (33°) stands for **self-limitation**: "Guard the house so well that it never needs you." See [[verifiable-lottery]], [[founder-sunset]].
- **Virtues against capture.** Restraint (9°), tolerance (10°), equity (11°), equality (29°), vigilance (30°), impartiality (31°) and balance (32°) form an anti-capture curriculum, consistent with the thesis that power accumulates where secrecy, discretion and permanence meet (see [[eternal-landmarks-of-openmasonry]], [[enlightenment-sociability]]).
- **Time as a cost.** Waiting periods grow in the higher Houses (365 days for the 19°, 31° and 32°; 730 days in the 32° to stand for the 33°). See [[waiting-period]].
