# Timeline of Freemasonry

A chronological map that gives the **status** of each claim. **Legend** (internal narrative, not history) and **disputed** are always flagged. Details are on the source and entity pages. Items marked **†** come from well-established general historical knowledge and do not yet have a clipped source in `raw/sources/`; they are candidates for ingestion.

## 1. Operative roots (14th–17th centuries)

| Date | Event | Status |
|---|---|---|
| "10th century" | Assembly of York under King Athelstan, said to have given the masons their rules | **Legend** (told in the Regius Poem) |
| c. 1390 – c. 1450 | **Regius Poem**, the oldest of the [[old-charges]] | Date **disputed**: 1390 is the traditional date, c. 1425–50 the palaeographic one. See [[hist-regius-poem]] |
| c. 1425–1450 † | Cooke Manuscript, the first Old Charge in prose | Approximate date |
| 1583 | [[william-schaw]] is appointed Master of Works to James VI | Fact |
| Dec 28, 1598 and Dec 28, 1599 | **Schaw Statutes**: wardens elected annually and written records | Fact. See [[hist-schaw-statutes-stevenson]] |
| 1599 → | Earliest surviving Scottish **lodge minutes** | Fact; which is "the oldest" varies |
| 1646 † | Elias Ashmole records his admission to a lodge at Warrington, one of the first English "accepted" masons | Fact (Ashmole's diary) |
| by 1710 | About 25 lodges in Scotland, with non-operative "gentlemen" | Thesis of [[david-stevenson]] |

## 3. Opening, exclusion and recognition (1826–1994)

| Date | Event | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1826 | Morgan Affair and the anti-Masonic wave in the US; the Southern Jurisdiction Supreme Council falls to 4 members by 1844 | Fact |
| 1856 / 1858 | [[albert-mackey]] publishes *Principles of Masonic Law* and the 25 [[landmarks]] | Fact; the landmarks were **never universally adopted** |
| 1859–1891 | [[albert-pike]] serves 32 years as Grand Commander; *Morals and Dogma* (1871/72) | Fact; the book's date varies |
| 1877–1878 | The Grand Orient de France adopts absolute liberty of conscience and the UGLE breaks off relations | Fact; origin of the regular vs. liberal divide |
| Jan 14, 1882 | Initiation of [[maria-deraismes]] | Fact |

| Nov 2026 | OpenMasonry whitepaper ("Genesis") | Independent project, no affiliation |

## Patterns the timeline reveals
1. **Writing before mystique:** the oldest documents are work regulations (Regius, Schaw), not secret rites.
2. **Cycles of schism and recognition:** Antients vs. Moderns, 1877, Prince Hall. Control of recognition has meant control of legitimacy.
3. **Opening from the margins:** women, Black men and non-theists came in through grand lodges that accepted losing recognition.
4. **Continuous decline** since 1959 in the US and since at least 2008 in England.
