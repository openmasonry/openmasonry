# Code of Conduct

OpenMasonry asks one thing of everyone who takes part, member or not: **judge acts, not persons, and yourself first.**

This code applies wherever the fraternity gathers: in the repository, in its discussions and in the rooms of study. A closed room is not a place apart from it ([rules of the rooms](docs/governance/ROOMS.md)).

## Expected

- Treat every person as a builder of equal dignity, whatever their sex, gender, origin, color, belief or unbelief, class, age or disability (Landmark I).
- Argue against ideas with evidence; never against people.
- Keep the privacy of others. Never publish someone's personal data.
- Disclose conflicts of interest before you examine, judge or vote.

## Not tolerated

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, retaliation for a vote or a verdict, vote buying or coercion, impersonation, deliberate falsification of records, binding or pressing anyone to silence about a room, and abuse of a correction. These are grave offenses under Constitution Art. X.

## Enforcement

Reports go to the moderators (members of the 10th degree and above) through a private GitHub security advisory marked as a conduct report ([SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md#conduct-reports) says how). Until members of the 10th degree exist, the Custodian moderates alone under the [Custodian's charter](docs/governance/CUSTODIAN.md). Where no custodian key is live and no member of the 10th degree exists, the owner of the repository on GitHub moderates under the same charter, and records nothing in the ledger until a custodian key is live again: a `note` needs that key, so each act is reported in a public issue instead.

- **Conversation.** A comment that harms someone may be hidden and a thread locked; a person who is not a member and abuses the repository may be blocked.
- **Members.** A member is sanctioned only by a ballot, under the graduated sanctions of Constitution Art. X: no moderator and no Custodian admonishes, suspends or expels anyone. A member is kept out of the rooms only by a room hold of 14 days, which is a correction (Constitution Art. XVI): a signed public entry with a ground and a public reason, to which Master Builders may object.
- **The record.** Every moderation act of the Custodian is recorded in the ledger as a `note` (while no custodian key is live, in a public issue, as above), with the action, its target and the rule applied, and never with the identity of the person who reported. The [moderation log](docs/governance/MODERATION-LOG.md) is the ledger itself, and anyone reads it with `bin/om corrections`.
- **Appeal.** Anyone affected may appeal in a public issue labeled `appeal`.

This code adapts the spirit of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
