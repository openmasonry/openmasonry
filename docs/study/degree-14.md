# 14°: Perfect Elect (study outline)

**House:** The Lodge of Perfection. Duty, stewardship and justice inside the community.  
**Virtue:** Integrity  
**Art:** Synthesis of the Perfection: the self as the first commons  
**Lesson:** "Integrity is the same person in the dark and in the light."

## Purpose

The 14th degree closes the Lodge of Perfection. It asks a member to show that the ten degrees before it formed one person: that what was studied as separate duties (discretion, memory, records, justice, stewardship, restraint, tolerance, equity, design, research) is practiced as a whole.

## Virtue: Integrity

Integrity is wholeness: being one person across contexts, with acts that match words. It is misread as perfection (people with integrity still err, and admit it) or as rigid consistency that refuses to learn.

## Art

Synthesis of the Perfection: the self as the first commons. The study treats one's own conduct as a shared resource that others depend on, and asks how the arts of degrees 4 to 13 apply to it.

## Lesson

> "Integrity is the same person in the dark and in the light."

The lesson gives a simple test: would the act be the same if no one could see it? A member who behaves well only when observed is managing a reputation, not keeping integrity.

## Masterwork

The canon states it exactly as follows:

> A public synthesis of degrees 4–13 and a service record: signed work entries or public records of service over at least 365 days

Practical pointers:

- Treat each of degrees 4 to 13 in turn: what was learned, what changed in practice, what remains hard.
- Connect them: show where two virtues pulled in different directions and how the conflict was handled.
- Document the service with signed `work` entries (`bin/om work <url>`) or other public records, spanning at least 365 days, with references to the work done, not with claims.
- Be candid about failures; integrity is shown in how they were handled.

**Procedure.** At least 30 days in the 13th degree; then five examiners, drawn from the members of the 14th degree and above, then from the Master Builders, as drawn by lot under the rules in [SPEC.md](../whitepaper/SPEC.md#73-the-examiner-pool-ladder), each publishing a reasoned verdict; then an advancement ballot of peers (steps in the [guide of the 3rd degree](degree-03.md#54-from-work-to-degree-the-examination-and-the-ballot)).

## Examination criteria

- All of degrees 4 to 13 are addressed and connected, not merely listed.
- The service record spans at least 365 days and rests on signed `work` entries or other verifiable public records.
- Stated values match the documented acts, and discrepancies are acknowledged.
- The work is the candidate's own; help and sources are acknowledged and traceable.

## Reading

- **Plato**, *Republic*, Book II, the story of the ring of Gyges (Jowett translation, public domain).
- *The Doctrine of the Mean* and *The Great Learning*, on watchfulness over oneself when alone (James Legge translation, public domain).
- **Marcus Aurelius**, *Meditations* (public-domain translations).
- **Michel de Montaigne**, *Essays*, "Of Repentance" (Charles Cotton translation, public domain).

## What it unlocks

The canon states: "Preside over a Lodge of Perfection". These are duties and eligibility, never weight (Landmark IV).
