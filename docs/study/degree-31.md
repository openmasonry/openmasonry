# 31°: Inspector Inquisitor, the Auditor (study outline)

**House:** The Consistory. Audit, judgment and the balance of powers.  
**Virtue:** Impartiality  
**Art:** Audit and judicial review: checking the powerful with their own rules  
**Lesson:** "Audit the auditors, beginning with yourself."  
**Summary page:** [docs/degrees/degree-31.md](../degrees/degree-31.md) (generated from [`canon/degrees.toml`](../../canon/degrees.toml))

An outline; the general criteria of every examination are in the [README](README.md).

## Purpose

The 31st degree opens the Consistory with the work of audit. Those who hold power in the fraternity are checked by members who examine their records against the rules. An Auditor has audited one House for a year and published the result, including disagreement.

## Virtue: Impartiality

Impartiality is judging by the same standard whoever is judged. It is misread as having no view; an impartial auditor reaches conclusions, by evidence, and discloses their own connections.

## Art

Audit and judicial review: checking the powerful with their own rules. The study covers audit scope and independence, sampling and full verification, evidence, findings and recommendations, the right of reply, and judicial review of decisions against higher rules.

## Lesson

> "Audit the auditors, beginning with yourself."

No auditor is above audit. The lesson asks auditors to begin by examining their own independence and methods, and to publish them so others can audit in turn.

## Masterwork

The canon states it exactly as follows:

> A full annual audit of one House, published with dissent sections

Practical pointers:

- Choose one House and one year, and declare any role held in it during that year.
- Check its records against the Constitution and the canon: degrees conferred, examinations, ballots, offices and accountability reports.
- Give those audited the chance to reply, and publish their replies.
- Include dissent sections where any auditor or audited party disagrees with a finding.

**Procedure.** At least 365 days in the 30th degree; then five examiners, drawn from the members of the 31st degree and above, then from the Master Builders, as drawn by lot under the rules in [SPEC.md](../whitepaper/SPEC.md#73-the-examiner-pool-ladder), each publishing a reasoned verdict; then an advancement ballot of peers (steps in the [guide of the 3rd degree](degree-03.md#54-from-work-to-degree-the-examination-and-the-ballot)).

## Examination criteria

- The audit covers the whole House for a full year.
- Findings are evidenced and tied to rules.
- Replies and dissent are published unedited.
- Conflicts of interest are disclosed.
- The work is the candidate's own; help and sources are acknowledged and traceable.

## Reading

- **INTOSAI**, *The Lima Declaration* on auditing principles (freely available).
- **Montesquieu**, *The Spirit of the Laws*, Book XI (public-domain translations).
- **Michael Power**, *The Audit Society* (widely available).
- **Juvenal**, *Satires*, VI, the origin of the question of who guards the guards (public-domain translations).

## What it unlocks

The canon states: "Sit on the Court of Review". These are duties and eligibility, never weight (Landmark IV).
