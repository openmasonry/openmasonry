# 21°: Noachite, Keeper of Humility (study outline)

**House:** The Council of Kadosh. Philosophy, law, and the defense of the commons against capture.  
**Virtue:** Humility before the law  
**Art:** Universal ethics and precedent: laws every person can accept  
**Lesson:** "The higher you stand, the lower the law must find you."

## Purpose

The 21st degree prepares members to keep the memory of judgments. A fraternity that is governed by public rules needs a public account of how those rules were applied, so that like cases are treated alike.

## Virtue: Humility before the law

Humility before the law is accepting that rules bind everyone, the powerful most of all. It is misread as deference to whoever enforces the law, or as the belief that rules should never change.

## Art

Universal ethics and precedent: laws every person can accept. The study covers the principle of universalizability, the rule of law, the role of precedent and the reasoning by analogy that applies it, how a holding is distinguished from passing remarks, and how precedent can be departed from openly.

## Lesson

> "The higher you stand, the lower the law must find you."

The lesson reverses the usual direction of privilege. The higher a member's degree or office, the more strictly the law applies to them.

## Masterwork

The canon states it exactly as follows:

> A precedent digest: summarize up to ten rulings from the public record, or all if fewer, and the principles they set

Practical pointers:

- Select up to ten rulings from the public record (ballots of sanctions, decisions of the Court of Review, corrections and their objections), or all of them where the record holds fewer, and state the source of each.
- For each ruling: the facts, the question, the outcome, the reasoning and the principle it set.
- Synthesize: where do the principles agree, conflict or leave gaps?
- Refer to persons by login at most, and include no personal data.

**Procedure.** At least 60 days in the 20th degree; then three examiners, drawn from the members of the 21st degree and above, then from the Master Builders, as drawn by lot under the rules in [SPEC.md](../whitepaper/SPEC.md#73-the-examiner-pool-ladder), each publishing a reasoned verdict; then an advancement ballot of peers (steps in the [guide of the 3rd degree](degree-03.md#54-from-work-to-degree-the-examination-and-the-ballot)).

## Examination criteria

- Up to ten rulings of the public record (all of them, where it holds fewer) are summarized accurately, each with its source.
- The principle drawn from each is supported by its reasoning.
- The synthesis identifies agreements, conflicts and gaps.
- The work is the candidate's own; help and sources are acknowledged and traceable.

## Reading

- **Immanuel Kant**, *Groundwork of the Metaphysics of Morals* (public-domain translations).
- **Lon L. Fuller**, *The Morality of Law* (widely available).
- **Edward H. Levi**, *An Introduction to Legal Reasoning* (widely available).
- **Cicero**, *On the Laws*, Book I (public-domain translations).

## What it unlocks

The canon states: "Maintain the book of precedents". These are duties and eligibility, never weight (Landmark IV).
