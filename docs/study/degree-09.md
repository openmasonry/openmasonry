## Purpose

The 9th degree prepares members to respond to attacks and failures without becoming what they fight. Incidents create pressure to act fast and hard; a member of the incident rota must act fast and proportionately.

## Virtue: Restraint

Restraint is the deliberate limitation of one's own response to what the situation requires. It is not passivity: it acts, but no more than needed, with evidence, and with a way to undo. It is misread as weakness, or as delay that lets harm continue.

## Art

Incident response: detection, triage, containment, eradication, recovery and review. The study covers runbooks, roles during an incident, communication, evidence preservation, the blameless review, and the difference between repairing a system and punishing a person. The project's runbooks are in [RECOVERY.md](../operations/RECOVERY.md) and [SECURITY.md](../../SECURITY.md).

## Lesson

> "Zeal without restraint is the first apprentice of tyranny."

Zeal that does not limit itself grants itself exceptions: it suspends rules for the emergency and then forgets to restore them. The lesson treats unrestrained zeal as the training ground of tyranny.

## Masterwork

The canon states it exactly as follows:

> Write or improve an incident-response runbook and run a tabletop exercise

Practical pointers:

- Choose an incident type the project could face and that existing runbooks cover poorly.
- Write the runbook with roles, decision points, proportionality checks, communication steps and the conditions for standing down.
- Run a tabletop exercise with several participants, and publish an after-action review without personal data.
- Do not disclose any unrepaired flaw in the runbook or the review.

**Procedure.** At least 30 days in the 8th degree; then three examiners, drawn from the members of the 9th degree and above, then from the Master Builders, as drawn by lot under the rules in [SPEC.md](../whitepaper/SPEC.md#73-the-examiner-pool-ladder), each publishing a reasoned verdict; then an advancement ballot of peers (steps in the [guide of the 3rd degree](degree-03.md#54-from-work-to-degree-the-examination-and-the-ballot)).

## Examination criteria

- The runbook is usable under pressure: clear steps, roles and decision points.
- Every forceful action in it is bounded, reversible where possible and tied to a stated rule.
- The tabletop exercise took place and its review records what was learned and changed.
- No unrepaired flaw and no personal data is disclosed.
- The work is the candidate's own; help and sources are acknowledged and traceable.
