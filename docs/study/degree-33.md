# 33°: Sovereign Guardian (study outline)

**House:** The Supreme Council. Guardianship of the Constitution. Serves; never commands.  
**Virtue:** Self-limitation  
**Art:** Guardianship of the Constitution  
**Lesson:** "Guard the house so well that it never needs you."  
**Summary page:** [docs/degrees/degree-33.md](../degrees/degree-33.md) (generated from [`canon/degrees.toml`](../../canon/degrees.toml))

An outline; the general criteria of every examination are in the [README](README.md).

## Purpose

The 33rd degree is not a reward and cannot be sought. It is conferred by verifiable lot on members of the 32nd degree who have held it long enough and have never sat, for a single term on the Supreme Council, whose powers are deliberately few (Constitution [Art. VIII](../../CONSTITUTION.md#article-viii-the-supreme-council)).

## Virtue: Self-limitation

Self-limitation is the restraint of one's own power by one's own choice, beyond what the rules require. It is misread as false modesty, or as refusal to act when action is a duty.

## Art

Guardianship of the Constitution: keeping the house within its own rules. The study covers the Council's limited powers (to delay a passed decision and refer it to the Court of Review, to convene an extraordinary assembly, to certify mirrors), what it may never do (admit, promote, sanction, spend, legislate or appoint), and the history of guardians who exceeded their mandate.

## Lesson

> "Guard the house so well that it never needs you."

The best guardianship is the one that is seldom needed. The lesson asks guardians to strengthen the house's own defenses so that their intervention becomes unnecessary.

## Masterwork

The canon states it exactly as follows:

> Not applied for. Drawn by verifiable lot from all eligible 32nd-degree members (see Whitepaper §7.5)

Practical pointers:

- Nothing is submitted: there is no masterwork and no examination.
- Eligibility is set by the canon and the specification: the 32nd degree held for at least 730 days, active and not dormant, and no seat ever held.
- Any active member may call the lot when seats are vacant; the eligible members are fixed at the call.
- Preparation is continued service and study of Art. VIII, so that a seat, if drawn, is held within its limits.

**Procedure.** No examiners are drawn. The lot is called and drawn as described in [Whitepaper §7.5](../whitepaper/WHITEPAPER.md#75-the-supreme-council) and [SPEC §6.12 and §6.13](../whitepaper/SPEC.md#612-council_call). A seat lasts three years and cannot be held twice.

## Examination criteria

There is no examination. A seated Guardian is judged afterwards, in public, by acts:

- Did every act of the Council stay within the powers of Art. VIII?
- Was each act reasoned and published?
- Did the Guardian vote in ordinary ballots like any other member, claiming no weight?

## What it unlocks

The canon states: "A three-year, non-renewable seat on the Supreme Council". These are duties and eligibility, never weight (Landmark IV).
