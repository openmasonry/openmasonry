## Purpose

The 32nd degree completes the path that can be applied for. A Master of the Royal Secret has written and publicly defended a thesis on how OpenMasonry's powers balance one another, and after 730 days in the degree enters the pool from which the Supreme Council is drawn.

## Virtue: Balance

Balance is the holding of opposing forces in a stable relation, without letting one absorb the others. It is misread as compromise for its own sake, or as inaction.

## Art

Synthesis: the balance of powers and the equilibrium of opposites. The study covers separation of powers, checks and balances, mixed constitutions, sortition, term limits, and the forces that pull institutions out of balance. OpenMasonry's own design is in Constitution [Art. II](../../CONSTITUTION.md#article-ii-separation-of-powers) and [Art. VIII](../../CONSTITUTION.md#article-viii-the-supreme-council).

## Lesson

> "The royal secret is that no one should hold it alone."

The degree's name recalls a 'royal secret'. The lesson defines it as the opposite of a secret: power that no single person or body may hold alone.

## Masterwork

The canon states it exactly as follows:

> A thesis on the balance of powers in OpenMasonry, defended publicly: written questions and answers in its public thread, never in a room

Practical pointers:

- Analyze each power of the fraternity (legislative, executive, judicial, guardianship, and the interim office of Custodian) and how the others check it.
- Identify failure modes: where balance could break, and what would restore it.
- Compare with at least two other institutions from different eras or regions.
- Defend the thesis in writing in its public thread (for example the comments on the published thesis), answering every question examiners ask, and never in a room.

**Procedure.** At least 365 days in the 31st degree; then seven examiners (fewer where fewer can be drawn: where the Master Builders other than the candidate are six, all six), drawn from the members of the 32nd degree and above, then from the Master Builders, as drawn by lot under the rules in [SPEC.md](../whitepaper/SPEC.md#73-the-examiner-pool-ladder), each publishing a reasoned verdict; then an advancement ballot of peers (steps in the [guide of the 3rd degree](degree-03.md#54-from-work-to-degree-the-examination-and-the-ballot)).

## Examination criteria

- Every power and its checks are analyzed accurately against the Constitution and the specification.
- Failure modes are identified with reasons.
- The public defense answered the questions raised.
- The work is the candidate's own; help and sources are acknowledged and traceable.

## Reading

- **Polybius**, *Histories*, Book VI, on the mixed constitution (public-domain translations).
- **Montesquieu**, *The Spirit of the Laws* (public-domain translations).
- **Heraclitus**, fragments, on the unity of opposites (public-domain translations).
- **Elinor Ostrom**, writings on polycentric governance (widely available).
- **Ibn Khaldun**, *The Muqaddimah*, on the cycles of dynasties (widely available).

## What it unlocks

The canon states: "Enter the sortition pool of the Supreme Council after 730 days in the degree". The Council lot is described in [Whitepaper §7.5](../whitepaper/WHITEPAPER.md#75-the-supreme-council). These are duties and eligibility, never weight (Landmark IV).
