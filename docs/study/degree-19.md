## Purpose

The 19th degree opens the Council of Kadosh with the work of connection. OpenMasonry is meant to grow as a federation of Lodges and independent witness nodes, not as a single center. A Pontifex has joined a new part to the whole and proved, by tests, that the parts agree.

## Virtue: Concord

Concord is agreement reached and kept among parties who remain distinct. It is misread as uniformity, or as peace bought by ignoring real differences.

## Art

Federation: interoperability between Lodges, nodes and other communities. The study covers shared protocols and their specifications, conformance testing, the robustness of systems built by independent parties, federated governance, and the trade-off between local autonomy and common rules. The [specification](../whitepaper/SPEC.md) and its conformance vectors define what two verifiers must agree on.

## Lesson

> "Build bridges you are willing to cross in both directions."

A bridge that carries traffic one way only is a gate. The lesson asks that every link between communities be equally open in both directions and equally binding on both sides.

## Masterwork

The canon states it exactly as follows:

> Federate a witness node, or a new Lodge when lodges exist, with interoperability tests

Practical pointers:

- Today the path is a witness node, added by witness ballot (Constitution [Art. XI.2](../../CONSTITUTION.md#article-xi-witnesses-the-tyler-and-infrastructure)); the federation of a new Lodge becomes possible only when lodges exist ([Art. IX.1](../../CONSTITUTION.md#article-ix-lodges-and-offices)).
- Write interoperability tests that show the new node or Lodge reaches the same state as the others from the same record.
- Publish the tests and their results, and document how others can repeat them.
- Document what the new part may decide locally and what it must share.

**Procedure.** At least 365 days in the 18th degree; then three examiners, drawn from the members of the 19th degree and above, then from the Master Builders, as drawn by lot under the rules in [SPEC.md](../whitepaper/SPEC.md#73-the-examiner-pool-ladder), each publishing a reasoned verdict; then an advancement ballot of peers (steps in the [guide of the 3rd degree](degree-03.md#54-from-work-to-degree-the-examination-and-the-ballot)).

## Examination criteria

- The node or Lodge was federated through the lawful process.
- Interoperability tests exist, are published and pass.
- Others can repeat the tests from the documentation.
- The work is the candidate's own; help and sources are acknowledged and traceable.

## Reading

- **Immanuel Kant**, *Perpetual Peace* (public-domain translations), on a federation of free states.
- **Pierre-Joseph Proudhon**, *The Principle of Federation* (widely available).
- **B. Carpenter, ed.**, *Architectural Principles of the Internet*, RFC 1958 (open).
- **IETF**, *The Tao of IETF* (open).

## What it unlocks

The canon states: "Approve federation of new Lodges, when lodges exist". These are duties and eligibility, never weight (Landmark IV).
