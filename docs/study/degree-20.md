## Purpose

The 20th degree prepares members to teach. A Master of the Symbolic Lodge writes curricula and trains examiners, so the degree asks for an improved course of study and the understanding of how adults learn.

## Virtue: Patience

Patience is the willingness to let learning take the time it takes. It is misread as passivity, or as low expectations; a patient teacher expects much and waits for it.

## Art

Pedagogy: how adults learn, and how to teach without dominating. The study covers self-directed learning, the role of experience, practice and feedback, the design of exercises and assessments aligned with stated goals, accessibility, and the difference between helping a learner and doing the work for them.

## Lesson

> "The master's work is complete when the apprentice no longer needs the master."

A teacher who makes themselves indispensable has failed. The lesson sets the goal of teaching as the learner's independence.

## Masterwork

The canon states it exactly as follows:

> Design or substantially improve one degree's study curriculum

Practical pointers:

- Choose one degree whose guide is still an outline, or a full guide that can be substantially improved.
- Align everything with the canon: the guide may explain the requirement, never add to it.
- Include exercises, a reading list of freely available sources and checkable criteria, as the [README](README.md) describes.
- Test the material with learners and revise from their feedback.
- Submit it to the repository through normal review.

**Procedure.** At least 60 days in the 19th degree; then three examiners, drawn from the members of the 20th degree and above, then from the Master Builders, as drawn by lot under the rules in [SPEC.md](../whitepaper/SPEC.md#73-the-examiner-pool-ladder), each publishing a reasoned verdict; then an advancement ballot of peers (steps in the [guide of the 3rd degree](degree-03.md#54-from-work-to-degree-the-examination-and-the-ballot)).

## Examination criteria

- The curriculum covers the purpose, virtue, art, lesson, masterwork and criteria of its degree.
- It is accurate to the canon and the specification, and adds no requirement.
- Its exercises and criteria are clear, fair and checkable.
- The work is the candidate's own; help and sources are acknowledged and traceable.

## Reading

- **John Amos Comenius**, *The Great Didactic* (public-domain translation).
- **John Dewey**, *Democracy and Education* (public domain, on Project Gutenberg).
- **Maria Montessori**, *The Montessori Method* (public domain, on Project Gutenberg).
- **Malcolm Knowles and others**, *The Adult Learner* (widely available).
- **Jacques Rancière**, *The Ignorant Schoolmaster* (widely available).

## What it unlocks

The canon states: "Author curricula; train examiners". These are duties and eligibility, never weight (Landmark IV).
