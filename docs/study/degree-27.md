# 27°: Commander of the Temple (study outline)

**Lesson:** "Command is a debt you pay to those who follow."

## Purpose

The 27th degree is the degree of leadership without privilege. A Commander of the Temple has held elected office through a full term and accounted for it in public, and may then stand for office at the level of a House.

## Virtue: Servant leadership

Servant leadership is leading in order to serve those led, with authority used for their benefit and returned at the end of the term. It is misread as humility that avoids decisions, or as a softer name for command.

## Art

Leadership: responsibility without privilege, authority without ownership. The study covers mandates and their limits, delegation, decision-making in the open, accountability, handover and the temptations of office.

## Lesson

> "Command is a debt you pay to those who follow."

Those who follow a leader lend their trust. The lesson treats command as the obligation that loan creates, paid in service and accounting.

## Masterwork

The canon states it exactly as follows:

> Hold an elected office for a full term with a public accountability report

Practical pointers:

- Hold an elected office for its full term; Lodge offices last one year (Constitution [Art. IX.2](../../CONSTITUTION.md#article-ix-lodges-and-offices)).
- Keep a record during the term of commitments, decisions and their reasons.
- Publish the accountability report at the end of the term (Art. IX.3): what was promised, what was done, what failed, what is handed over.

**Procedure.** At least 60 days in the 26th degree; then three examiners, drawn from the members of the 27th degree and above, then from the Master Builders, as drawn by lot under the rules in [SPEC.md](../whitepaper/SPEC.md#73-the-examiner-pool-ladder), each publishing a reasoned verdict; then an advancement ballot of peers (steps in the [guide of the 3rd degree](degree-03.md#54-from-work-to-degree-the-examination-and-the-ballot)).

## Examination criteria

- The full term was served.
- The report is public, complete and honest about failures.
- Decisions are explained with their reasons.
- The work is the candidate's own; help and sources are acknowledged and traceable.

## Reading

- **Laozi**, *Tao Te Ching*, chapter 17 (James Legge translation, public domain).
- **Robert K. Greenleaf**, *The Servant as Leader* (widely available).
- **Plutarch**, *Parallel Lives* (public-domain translations).
- **Xenophon**, *Cyropaedia* (public-domain translations).

## What it unlocks

The canon states: "Stand for House-level office". These are duties and eligibility, never weight (Landmark IV).
