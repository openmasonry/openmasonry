## Purpose

The 26th degree prepares members to repair and to welcome back. Justice that only excludes leaves wounds open; a Prince of Mercy facilitates repair and gives those who ask to return a fair hearing.

## Virtue: Mercy

Mercy is the choice to temper a deserved consequence for the sake of repair and of the person. It is misread as forgetting harm, or as forgiveness demanded from those who were harmed.

## Art

Restorative justice: repair, amnesty and the return of the excluded. The study covers the needs of those harmed, accountability of those who caused harm, voluntary participation, facilitation, agreements, reintegration, and when a restorative process is not appropriate. The Constitution provides for the return of the expelled and a hearing by a Prince of Mercy ([Art. X.4](../../CONSTITUTION.md#article-x-justice-sanctions-and-mercy)).

## Lesson

> "Justice that cannot forgive is only accounting."

Justice that only counts wrongs and assigns penalties keeps the books balanced without healing anything. The lesson asks justice to leave room for forgiveness.

## Masterwork

The canon states it exactly as follows:

> Facilitate one restorative process or reinstatement review

Practical pointers:

- Facilitate only with the voluntary participation of everyone involved, and stop if any party withdraws.
- Prepare each party separately before any joint meeting.
- Stay impartial; declare any connection to a party.
- Document only what the parties agree may be public, with no personal data.

**Procedure.** At least 60 days in the 25th degree; then three examiners, drawn from the members of the 26th degree and above, then from the Master Builders, as drawn by lot under the rules in [SPEC.md](../whitepaper/SPEC.md#73-the-examiner-pool-ladder), each publishing a reasoned verdict; then an advancement ballot of peers (steps in the [guide of the 3rd degree](degree-03.md#54-from-work-to-degree-the-examination-and-the-ballot)).

## Examination criteria

- The process was voluntary and fairly facilitated.
- The needs of those harmed were central.
- The documentation respects privacy and the parties' agreement.
- The work is the candidate's own; help and sources are acknowledged and traceable.

## Reading

- **Seneca**, *On Mercy* (public-domain translations).
- **Howard Zehr**, *The Little Book of Restorative Justice* (widely available).
- **United Nations Economic and Social Council**, *Basic Principles on the Use of Restorative Justice Programmes in Criminal Matters* (freely available).
- **John Braithwaite**, *Crime, Shame and Reintegration* (widely available).
- **Hannah Arendt**, *The Human Condition*, the section on forgiveness (widely available).

## What it unlocks

The canon states: "Grant clemency petitions a hearing". Constitution Art. X.4 asks a Prince of Mercy to grant the request to return a hearing within 60 days. These are duties and eligibility, never weight (Landmark IV).
