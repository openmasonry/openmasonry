# 18°: Knight of the Rose (study outline)

**House:** The Chapter of the Rose. Rebuilding, liberty of conscience and active charity.  
**Virtue:** Charity  
**Art:** Active benevolence: mentoring, relief and the ethics of care  
**Lesson:** "Love that does not act is only a feeling about oneself."  
**Summary page:** [docs/degrees/degree-18.md](../degrees/degree-18.md) (generated from [`canon/degrees.toml`](../../canon/degrees.toml))

An outline; the general criteria of every examination are in the [README](README.md).

## Purpose

The 18th degree closes the Chapter of the Rose with charity in action. A Knight of the Rose has helped two newer members grow and has led an act of relief for people in need, and so is trusted to preside over a Chapter and administer relief.

## Virtue: Charity

Charity is love expressed in effective action for others' good. It is misread as pity, as giving that serves the giver's image, or as help imposed without the consent of those helped.

## Art

Active benevolence: mentoring, relief and the ethics of care. The study covers mentoring that builds independence, the dignity and consent of those who receive help, effectiveness, avoiding dependency, and the care of those who give care.

## Lesson

> "Love that does not act is only a feeling about oneself."

Love that stays a feeling helps no one but the one who feels it. The lesson measures charity by what it does for the other person.

## Masterwork

The canon states it exactly as follows:

> Mentor two Apprentices to the degree of Fellow and lead one relief action

Practical pointers:

- Mentor two Apprentices through to the 2nd degree without doing their work: authorship stays theirs.
- Recuse if drawn as examiner of a mentee, and never canvass their examination.
- Plan the relief action with those it helps, with their consent, and protect their privacy.
- Document both parts without personal data, and with the mentees' agreement.

**Procedure.** At least 60 days in the 17th degree; then five examiners, drawn from the members of the 18th degree and above, then from the Master Builders, as drawn by lot under the rules in [SPEC.md](../whitepaper/SPEC.md#73-the-examiner-pool-ladder), each publishing a reasoned verdict; then an advancement ballot of peers (steps in the [guide of the 3rd degree](degree-03.md#54-from-work-to-degree-the-examination-and-the-ballot)).

## Examination criteria

- Two mentees reached the 2nd degree with the candidate's documented mentoring.
- The mentoring built the mentees' independence rather than dependence.
- The relief action took place, was led by the candidate and respected the dignity and privacy of those helped.
- The work is the candidate's own; help and sources are acknowledged and traceable.

## Reading

- **Seneca**, *On Benefits* (public-domain translations).
- **Peter Kropotkin**, *Mutual Aid: A Factor of Evolution* (public domain).
- **Maimonides**, the eight levels of giving, in the *Mishneh Torah* (widely available).
- **Nel Noddings**, *Caring* (widely available).

## What it unlocks

The canon states: "Preside over a Chapter of the Rose; administer the relief fund". These are duties and eligibility, never weight (Landmark IV).
