## Purpose

The 13th degree is the degree of research. It asks a member to answer a question by going to the sources and digging, patiently and honestly, until the evidence supports a conclusion or shows that none is yet possible.

## Virtue: Perseverance

Perseverance is continuing a hard task through difficulty and slow progress. In research it is the patience to read the whole source, to check the citation behind the citation, and to report a negative result. It is misread as stubbornness that ignores contrary evidence.

## Art

Research: first principles, primary sources and method. The study covers forming a researchable question, searching and evaluating literature, working with primary sources, choosing and stating a method, the limits of evidence, reproducibility, and the ethics of research involving people.

## Lesson

> "What is buried is not lost to the one who keeps digging honestly."

Truth that was buried (by time, neglect or design) can be recovered by one who keeps digging and does not invent what is missing. Honesty is the condition: a digger who plants what they hope to find has found nothing.

## Masterwork

The canon states it exactly as follows:

> A research paper with primary sources, examined as drawn by lot

Practical pointers:

- State one researchable question and why it matters.
- Rely on primary sources, cited precisely enough for a reader to find each one.
- Describe the method so that someone else could repeat it, and publish any data used.
- State the limits of the conclusion and what evidence would change it.

**Procedure.** At least 30 days in the 12th degree; then three examiners, drawn from the members of the 13th degree and above, then from the Master Builders, as drawn by lot under the rules in [SPEC.md](../whitepaper/SPEC.md#73-the-examiner-pool-ladder), each publishing a reasoned verdict; then an advancement ballot of peers (steps in the [guide of the 3rd degree](degree-03.md#54-from-work-to-degree-the-examination-and-the-ballot)).

## Examination criteria

- The paper answers a stated question with a stated method.
- Its key claims rest on primary sources, accurately cited.
- Limits and contrary evidence are addressed.
- The work is the candidate's own; help and sources are acknowledged and traceable.

## Reading

- **Francis Bacon**, *Novum Organum* (public-domain translations).
- **René Descartes**, *Discourse on the Method* (public domain, on Project Gutenberg).
- **Santiago Ramón y Cajal**, *Advice for a Young Investigator* (widely available).
- **Umberto Eco**, *How to Write a Thesis* (widely available).
- **Marc Bloch**, *The Historian's Craft* (widely available).

## What it unlocks

The canon states: "Supervise research circles". These are duties and eligibility, never weight (Landmark IV).
