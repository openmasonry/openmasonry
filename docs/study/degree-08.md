# 8°: Intendant of the Works (study outline)

**House:** The Lodge of Perfection. Duty, stewardship and justice inside the community.  
**Virtue:** Diligence in service  
**Art:** Stewardship of shared work: planning, maintenance and delegation  
**Lesson:** "A builder who will not maintain is only a visitor."  
**Summary page:** [docs/degrees/degree-08.md](../degrees/degree-08.md) (generated from [`canon/degrees.toml`](../../canon/degrees.toml))

An outline; the general criteria of every examination are in the [README](README.md).

## Purpose

The 8th degree turns the builder into a maintainer. Much of the value of a commons lies in the unglamorous work of keeping it running: answering issues, reviewing changes, fixing what breaks, handing over well. An Intendant of the Works has done that work for a sustained period.

## Virtue: Diligence in service

Diligence in service is the steady care of something shared, on behalf of others, over time. It is misread as control (maintainers serve the resource, they do not own it), as heroic overwork, or as availability without limits.

## Art

Stewardship of shared work: planning, maintenance and delegation. The study covers triage, review practice, release and change management, documentation, the bus factor (how many people can disappear before work stops), sustainable pace and the handover of responsibilities.

## Lesson

> "A builder who will not maintain is only a visitor."

A visitor builds and leaves. A builder stays to repair what was built. The lesson rejects the habit of valuing only new work.

## Masterwork

The canon states it exactly as follows:

> Maintain one repository, catalog or study circle for 90 days

Practical pointers:

- Agree the maintainer role publicly with those responsible for the resource before the 90 days begin.
- Keep a public log of maintenance acts: issues triaged, reviews given, releases, fixes, decisions.
- Delegate and document, so that at least one other person could continue the work.
- End with a short maintenance report: state at the start, state at the end, open problems and a handover note.

**Procedure.** At least 30 days in the 7th degree; then three examiners, drawn from the members of the 8th degree and above, then from the Master Builders, as drawn by lot under the rules in [SPEC.md](../whitepaper/SPEC.md#73-the-examiner-pool-ladder), each publishing a reasoned verdict; then an advancement ballot of peers (steps in the [guide of the 3rd degree](degree-03.md#54-from-work-to-degree-the-examination-and-the-ballot)).

## Examination criteria

- Maintenance was continuous over at least 90 days, as the public log shows.
- The resource is in a better or at least sustained state, by stated measures.
- The work can be continued by someone else from the documentation left.
- The report is honest about what was not done.
- The work is the candidate's own; help and sources are acknowledged and traceable.

## Reading

- **Karl Fogel**, *Producing Open Source Software* (open, freely available).
- **Nadia Eghbal**, *Working in Public* (widely available).
- **Eric S. Raymond**, *The Cathedral and the Bazaar* (freely available).
- **Frederick P. Brooks Jr.**, *The Mythical Man-Month* (widely available).

## What it unlocks

The canon states: "Maintain a commons repository". Maintainers (8°+) hold part of the executive functions under Constitution [Art. II.2](../../CONSTITUTION.md#article-ii-separation-of-powers). These are duties and eligibility, never weight (Landmark IV).
