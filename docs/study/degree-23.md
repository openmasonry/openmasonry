## Purpose

The 23rd degree serves the shared life of the fraternity through ceremony. OpenMasonry's ceremonies are original, public texts ([Whitepaper §11.3](../whitepaper/WHITEPAPER.md#113-ceremonies)), and a Servant of Rites writes and revises them.

## Virtue: Reverence

Reverence is respect for what deserves it, expressed in attention and care. It is misread as solemnity for its own sake, or as attachment to forms whose meaning is lost.

## Art

Ceremony design: ritual as shared meaning and as user experience. The study covers what ceremonies do (mark transitions, bind a group, teach), how symbol, words, movement and space carry meaning, inclusion of participants of any belief or unbelief, accessibility, and how to test a ceremony with real participants.

## Lesson

> "A rite that no longer teaches has become a costume."

A ceremony is alive while it teaches those who take part. When it is repeated only because it always was, it has become a costume. The lesson is a standard for revising any rite.

## Masterwork

The canon states it exactly as follows:

> Write or revise an open ceremony (public, original text)

Practical pointers:

- Write wholly original text. Never reproduce or paraphrase any ritual, obligation, password, sign, grip, word or catechism of any Masonic or other body.
- Include no oath of secrecy: binding anyone to silence is a grave offense (Constitution [Art. X.2](../../CONSTITUTION.md#article-x-justice-sanctions-and-mercy)).
- Make the ceremony open to people of any belief or unbelief (Landmark I), and accessible.
- State the purpose of the ceremony and how each part serves it.
- Rehearse it with a group and revise from their response.

**Procedure.** At least 60 days in the 22nd degree; then three examiners, drawn from the members of the 23rd degree and above, then from the Master Builders, as drawn by lot under the rules in [SPEC.md](../whitepaper/SPEC.md#73-the-examiner-pool-ladder), each publishing a reasoned verdict; then an advancement ballot of peers (steps in the [guide of the 3rd degree](degree-03.md#54-from-work-to-degree-the-examination-and-the-ballot)).

## Examination criteria

- The text is original and contains no material of any other body.
- It contains no obligation of secrecy and excludes no one by belief, origin or ability.
- Each part serves the stated purpose.
- The work is the candidate's own; help and sources are acknowledged and traceable.

## Reading

- **Arnold van Gennep**, *The Rites of Passage* (widely available).
- **Émile Durkheim**, *The Elementary Forms of Religious Life* (public-domain translation).
- **Xunzi**, "Discourse on Ritual" (widely available).
- **Victor Turner**, *The Ritual Process* (widely available).
- **Priya Parker**, *The Art of Gathering* (widely available).

## What it unlocks

The canon states: "Edit the open ritual book". These are duties and eligibility, never weight (Landmark IV).
