## Purpose

The 29th degree guards the open door. Landmark I forbids refusing or ranking anyone for sex, gender, origin, color, belief or unbelief, class or disability; a Knight of Saint Andrew checks that the path into the fraternity keeps that promise in practice.

## Virtue: Equality

Equality is equal standing and equal consideration for every person. It is misread as identical treatment that ignores real barriers, or as a goal reached once and for all.

## Art

Accessibility and non-discrimination: the open door kept open. The study covers accessibility standards for digital tools, barriers of language, cost, technology and knowledge, indirect discrimination, and audit methods that involve the people affected.

## Lesson

> "Every locked door was once called a tradition."

Exclusions often survive by being called customs. The lesson asks that every barrier be examined on its merits, however old it is.

## Masterwork

The canon states it exactly as follows:

> An accessibility and inclusion audit of the whole admission path

Practical pointers:

- Walk the whole admission path: the application, the invitation, the petition and its tools, the ballot.
- Test it against an accessibility standard and with people who use assistive technology, where they agree to take part.
- Look for barriers of language, cost, technology and assumed knowledge, as well as rules.
- Rank findings by severity and file each one, with a proposed remedy.

**Procedure.** At least 60 days in the 28th degree; then three examiners, drawn from the members of the 29th degree and above, then from the Master Builders, as drawn by lot under the rules in [SPEC.md](../whitepaper/SPEC.md#73-the-examiner-pool-ladder), each publishing a reasoned verdict; then an advancement ballot of peers (steps in the [guide of the 3rd degree](degree-03.md#54-from-work-to-degree-the-examination-and-the-ballot)).

## Examination criteria

- The audit covers the whole path, step by step.
- Findings are evidenced and rated by a stated standard.
- Remedies are concrete and the findings were filed.
- The work is the candidate's own; help and sources are acknowledged and traceable.

## What it unlocks

The canon states: "Raise an equality objection: refer to the Court of Review any rule that refuses or ranks anyone for sex, gender, origin, color, belief or unbelief, class or disability". These are duties and eligibility, never weight (Landmark IV).
