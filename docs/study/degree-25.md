## Purpose

The 25th degree is the degree of care for members' wellbeing. Volunteer communities wear people out; a Healer builds the processes that notice distress, offer support and connect people to qualified help.

## Virtue: Compassion

Compassion is the recognition of another's suffering joined to the will to relieve it. It is misread as taking on others' burdens without limit, or as offering advice instead of listening.

## Art

Wellbeing: mental health, burnout and care inside communities. The study covers the signs of distress and burnout, listening, psychological first aid, the limits of peer support and when to refer to professionals, privacy and consent, and the care of those who support others.

## Lesson

> "Heal the one in front of you before you theorize about the many."

Theories about communities are useful only if they lead to help for actual people. The lesson puts the person in front of you first.

## Masterwork

The canon states it exactly as follows:

> Build or improve a wellbeing support process and train five members in it

Practical pointers:

- Design or improve a process with clear limits: peer support and referral, never treatment.
- Include ways to reach qualified help in different regions and languages.
- Protect privacy at every step; consent comes first.
- Train five members in the process and gather their feedback.
- Document the process and the training without personal data.

**Procedure.** At least 60 days in the 24th degree; then three examiners, drawn from the members of the 25th degree and above, then from the Master Builders, as drawn by lot under the rules in [SPEC.md](../whitepaper/SPEC.md#73-the-examiner-pool-ladder), each publishing a reasoned verdict; then an advancement ballot of peers (steps in the [guide of the 3rd degree](degree-03.md#54-from-work-to-degree-the-examination-and-the-ballot)).

## Examination criteria

- The process exists in writing, with its limits stated.
- Five members were trained, and the training is documented.
- Privacy and consent are protected throughout.
- The work is the candidate's own; help and sources are acknowledged and traceable.

## Reading

- **World Health Organization**, *Psychological First Aid: Guide for Field Workers* (freely available).
- **World Health Organization**, the description of burn-out in ICD-11 (freely available).
- **Christina Maslach and Michael P. Leiter**, *The Truth About Burnout* (widely available).
- **Emily Nagoski and Amelia Nagoski**, *Burnout* (widely available).

## What it unlocks

The canon states: "Serve as wellbeing steward". These are duties and eligibility, never weight (Landmark IV).
