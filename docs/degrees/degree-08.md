# 8°: Intendant of the Works

<img src="../../heraldry/degree-08.svg" width="120" align="right">

**House:** The Lodge of Perfection. Duty, stewardship and justice inside the community.  
**Historical reference:** Intendant of the Building *(Ancient and Accepted Scottish Rite, Southern Jurisdiction; reference only; no ritual reproduced)*  
**Virtue:** Diligence in service  
**Art (field of study):** Stewardship of shared work: planning, maintenance and delegation

> A builder who will not maintain is only a visitor.

## Requirements

- **Masterwork:** Maintain one repository, catalog or study circle for 90 days
- **Waiting period:** at least 30 days in the previous degree
- **Examiners drawn by lot:** 3
- **From admission:** at least 390 days of waiting, plus the time the examinations and ballots on the way take

## What it unlocks

Maintain a commons repository. Degrees grant *duties and eligibility*, never extra votes (Landmark IV).

## Blazon

Field *vert*, ordinary *fess*, charge *plumb* (rendered as the degree numeral).
