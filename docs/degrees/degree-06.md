# 6°: Scribe of Trust

<img src="../../heraldry/degree-06.svg" width="120" align="right">

**House:** The Lodge of Perfection. Duty, stewardship and justice inside the community.  
**Historical reference:** Intimate Secretary *(Ancient and Accepted Scottish Rite, Southern Jurisdiction; reference only; no ritual reproduced)*  
**Virtue:** Faithfulness  
**Art (field of study):** Records and signatures: hashes, keys and the integrity of the written word

> Every record is a promise to someone who is not yet born.

## Requirements

- **Masterwork:** Independently verify the full ledger from genesis and publish the verification transcript
- **Waiting period:** at least 30 days in the previous degree
- **Examiners drawn by lot:** 3
- **From admission:** at least 330 days of waiting, plus the time the examinations and ballots on the way take

## What it unlocks

Operate a witness node, once added by a witness ballot. Degrees grant *duties and eligibility*, never extra votes (Landmark IV).

## Blazon

Field *azure*, ordinary *pale*, charge *quill* (rendered as the degree numeral).
