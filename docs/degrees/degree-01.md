# 1°: Apprentice of the Open Craft

<img src="../../heraldry/degree-01.svg" width="120" align="right">

**House:** The Craft Lodge. Self-knowledge and the tools of the work.  
**Historical reference:** Entered Apprentice *(Ancient and Accepted Scottish Rite, Southern Jurisdiction; reference only; no ritual reproduced)*  
**Virtue:** Humility  
**Art (field of study):** Grammar and Rhetoric: learning to read the work and speak clearly about it

> Before you shape the stone, learn to see the stone that you are.

## Requirements

- **Masterwork:** Signed declaration of the Landmarks (the petition); after admission, a first public reflection (500+ words) at a public URL the member controls, recorded as a work entry. No examination: the admission ballot confers the degree
- **Waiting period:** at least 0 days in the previous degree
- **Examiners drawn by lot:** 0
- **From admission:** conferred by the admission ballot itself

## What it unlocks

Speak in the Lodge, open issues, join study circles. Degrees grant *duties and eligibility*, never extra votes (Landmark IV).

## Blazon

Field *argent*, ordinary *plain*, charge *rough-ashlar* (rendered as the degree numeral).
